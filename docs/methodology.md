# Methodology

By Kai · Source: <https://earlylore.com/#method> · 中文: <https://earlylore.com/zh/>

A useful product research record answers three questions: **what was observed, when it was checked, and what the evidence can support.** This catalog applies that to a small, dated set of products.

## How a record is built

1. **Official source first.** Each product is identified by its own official website. The website URL is stored as a source record with the date it was observed.
2. **Our own words.** The summary and description are short notes written by EarlyLore, not copied marketing text.
3. **Pricing as a model, not a number.** `pricing` records only the pricing model seen on the official site (free, freemium, free trial or paid). No prices are stated in this snapshot.
4. **Categories.** Each product is placed in one or more of 7 use-case categories.
5. **Dates on everything.** `observedAt` and `verifiedAt` say when the record was checked. In this snapshot they are all 2026-07-28.

## What this catalog does and does not claim

- It records what was visible on each product's official site on the observation date.
- It does **not** rank products, judge quality or promise that a product or an idea will succeed.
- It does **not** state revenue, traffic or user numbers.
- An observation is kept separate from any conclusion drawn from it. If a field may be out of date, treat it as a lead and check the source.

## Limits

- This is a single snapshot. There is no change history in it yet, so it cannot show pricing or positioning changes.
- Records have not been re-verified since 2026-07-28.
- Coverage is a curated set of well-known AI products, not a complete market list.
